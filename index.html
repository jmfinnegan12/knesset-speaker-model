Where it stands

  • 2026-10-27: election. Netanyahu bloc 53 seats, Eisenkot bloc 48, Arab parties 19.1
  • 2026-11-08: President Herzog gives Gadi Eisenkot the mandate to form a government; deadline 2026-12-06, extendable by 14 days.2,5
  • 2026-11-10: the Knesset is sworn in without a Speaker vote. Amir Ohana (Likud) stays on as outgoing Speaker.3,6
  • 67 members ask for a vote. Ohana sets it for 2026-11-17, 16:00 Israel time. Candidate: Matan Kahana (Yashar).3,4

Nothing requires a vote by a fixed date. Two things force one: a majority demand, under the 2020 Supreme Court ruling in HCJ 2144/20,7 and the sitting that swears in a government, which must elect a Speaker first.6
