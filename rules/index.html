When will the Knesset elect a Speaker?

Question Model Rules

GitHub · suggest a change · how to contribute

Rules

Market: KXISRSPEAKERDATE on Kalshi, event KXISRSPEAKERDATE-27JAN01 · contract terms

  • Yes if the Knesset elected on Oct 27, 2026 elects a Speaker before the contract's date.
  • Counts: the first vote in which this Knesset elects a Speaker, including an interim or temporary Speaker or the re-election of the outgoing Speaker. A later replacement does not change the outcome.
  • Does not count: the outgoing Speaker continuing without a vote, a Deputy Speaker, a member presiding or acting as Speaker, a request for a vote, or the announcement or scheduling of one.
  • Contracts: before Nov 11 (settled No), Nov 13, Nov 18, Dec 1 and Jan 1. Each closes at 23:59 ET the day before its date, or early once a Speaker is elected.
  • Source: the Knesset.
